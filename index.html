Analysis settings
Steps up to “small road” = neighbourhood streets; 2× that = small connectors; up to 3 km = local roads; up to 20 km = N-road-like; longer = main road/motorway. One odd step inside a cluster is smoothed away (old bands). The new methods work on the log of the step lengths: “ratio ×” = how different two neighbouring road lengths may be and still count as the same kind of road. All four methods always run, so the Route anatomy tab can compare them — OSM probes are the reference.

How OSRM counts time — the demo server’s car profile (car.lua)

Speed per road type (km/h, used when a road has no speed limit tag): motorway 90 · trunk 85 · primary 65 · secondary 55 · tertiary 40 · unclassified 25 · residential 25 · living street 10 · service 15 · ramps: motorway link 45, trunk link 40, primary link 30, secondary link 25, tertiary link 20.
When a limit is tagged (maxspeed), the profile drives at 0.8 × the limit (“speed_reduction”). So a 100 road counts as 80, a 130 road as ~104, a 50 road as 40. Missing tags fall back to country defaults (urban 50, rural 90, trunk 110, motorway 130) — also ×0.8. That is why OSRM’s times look slow on fast roads and why this app corrects it by ×1.25 above ~60 km/h (setting “OSRM speed correction”).
Extras that add seconds: every turn gets a sigmoid penalty with base 7.5 s (turn bias 1.075, so right-hand and left-hand turns differ slightly), a U-turn +20 s, gates/barriers 60 s, side roads are driven at 0.8× on top, poor surface or smoothness caps the speed (gravel 40, cobblestone 30 …), ferries count 5 km/h. The list of turn penalties is why many short steps in a town cost more time than their length suggests.
What it does not know: live or historic traffic, time of day, real congestion, traffic light phases (lights only count through the turn penalty), and your vehicle. Result: the same duration at 03:00 and at 17:00. That is exactly why the clock in the Timeline uses our own speed model and only shows OSRM’s time next to it.
